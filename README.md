# Nena & Simba — Busy Days

A cozy time-management game for phones (PWA, installable, plays offline).
Tehran, a big modern apartment on the 5th floor: Nena takes care of Simba, her chow chow, and her whole family.

## How to play
- Every level is a day with a timer. Requests pop up with a patience ring:
  Simba (food, water, walk, brushing, play), Baba (tea), Maman (help in the kitchen), Nasim (talk), Negin (wake up).
- Tap things to send Nena: she queues up to 4 actions, carries items (kibble, tea, brush, ball) and works on stations.
- Fast chains give combos and tips. Missed requests cost hearts and make a mess (fur, puddles, dishes).
- Reach the score goals for 1 to 3 stars, earn coins, buy upgrades (sneakers, tray, big samovar, robot vacuum…).
- Short family scenes between days.

## Run
Serve the folder (`python3 -m http.server`) and open it, or install it from the browser on a phone.

## Files
`index.html`, `style.css`, `js/rush.js` (game), `js/art.js` (characters), `js/audio.js` (music and sounds), `manifest.webmanifest`, `sw.js`, `icons/`, `assets/`.

## Maps

- **Home** (Tehran, 6-floor tower): Living room, Kitchen, TV room, Bedroom hallway (Nena only), Nena's room with her bathroom. Use the room tabs at the bottom; tap an icon on a tab to send Nena there.
- **Tehran street**: bakery (sangak), juice shop, fountain, the family's white 4×4 and sedan.
- **CLAP** (Hayat Sabz mall, ground floor): Nena parks the 4×4 in the mall parking, takes the elevator up to the mall and walks to CLAP. Bamboo all around, wooden tables along the walls, Nena and Negin at the middle table, two blonde "witches" always sitting in the far corner, and Pourya, the hookah guy, who asks for Nena's Instagram every single time.

## Daily & progression

- Daily reward (7-day streak), daily challenge with a twist, 3 daily Color Sort puzzles (easy, medium, a bit hard), weekly missions.
- Family friendship levels with unlockable scenes, 16 trophies, a Simba photo album (tap the 📸 when Simba does something cute).
- Decorate the flat (decor shows up in the rooms), wardrobe for Nena and collars for Simba, Endless mode with a high score.
- Story chapters with ending scenes and a boss day: Cousin Sara's wedding. Surprise events: blackouts, Aunt Mahin, CLAP VIPs, the witches' curse, Simba the sangak thief.

## Life & feelings

- Faces change with patience (happy → neutral → angry), emotion icons (💢 ❤️ 😢 ❗ 💦), thank-you and grumpy lines, reactions to blackouts and gifts.
- Simba's happiness meter: care, play and cuddles raise it; neglect lowers it. Tap Simba to pet him (belly rubs, licks, high fives). He barks at the doorbell, hides during blackouts, comes back muddy from rainy walks, wears a party hat on his birthday and has a shiba friend, Biscuit.
- New activities: find Baba's lost remote (search the ❓ spots), water the plant, Simba's playdate with Biscuit, Negin's Instagram selfie at CLAP.
- Living decor: wall clock, birds, rain on the windows, twinkling city lights, a football match on the TV, fairy lights, pigeons and a cat in the street, birthday and wedding decorations.
- New mini-games: Simba's tricks (memory) and Tahdig master (timing), next to Color Sort and Nail day.

## Wellness & daily journey

- Today's journey: chests after 5, 10, 20 and 30 minutes of active play each day (the 30-minute chest unlocks the Zen linen outfit). Nothing ever stops the player from leaving.
- Daily meditation: Nena lights her oud, then breathes with the circle (hold to breathe in, release to breathe out). Gives a Zen bonus: the family is 10% more patient today.
- Sometimes Nena then goes for a walk and hugs a tree for good energy (+1 heart today).
- Hair day: after her shower, Maman dries Nena's hair. Sweep the dryer over the wet hair without burning her.
- The result screen shows what tomorrow brings.

## Reasons to come back

- Player level with XP from everything you do; each level gives coins, every 2 levels a wheel spin.
- Lucky wheel: a free spin every day, extra spins for perfect days, levels and the daily quest chest (coins, free boosters, sticker packs, jackpot).
- Sticker album: 24 stickers in 4 sets (family, Simba, Tehran, CLAP), dropped by finished days; complete a set for 100 coins.
- Daily quests (3 small tasks + a chest) next to the weekly missions.
- Welcome back: when you return after a few hours, Simba greets you with a gift.
